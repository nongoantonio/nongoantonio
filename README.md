<h2 align="center">💭 How I Think About Building Software</h2>

<p align="center" width="80%">
  Great software isn't just about making something work — it's about making it work
  <em>well</em>, for the people who actually use it. I approach every project by first
  understanding the problem deeply, then designing an interface that feels obvious, and
  only then writing the code to bring it to life. Design and engineering aren't separate
  disciplines to me — they're two sides of the same craft, and the best products come from
  treating them that way. I'd rather ship something smaller and well-considered than
  something large and rushed.
</p>
